# bug.sa

Saudi Arabia's bug bounty platform, for organizations and security researchers. **Launching soon.**

## What we're building

- **Bug bounty programs:** public, private or invite-only programs with your own scope, rules and reward ranges.
- **Triage and validation:** every report is reproduced, de-duplicated and rated by severity before it reaches your team.
- **Researcher payouts:** bounties paid to researchers in riyals, with payout history in one place.
- **Penetration testing:** time-boxed tests by vetted researchers for launches, audits and compliance.
- **CVE registration:** researchers request a CVE; we review it and publish it to the global CVE List, with their name in the credits.
- **Security advisories:** organizations subscribe with their tech stack and versions, and get alerted when a vulnerability affects them.

## Try it now

The [CVSS calculator](https://bug.sa/en/cvss) scores and compares CVSS 3.1 and 4.0 vectors side by side, in English and Arabic.
